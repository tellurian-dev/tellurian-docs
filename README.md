# Tellurian Documentation & Master Design Specification

Welcome to the central design and architecture repository for **Tellurian: A Living Planetary Sandbox**.

This repository contains the complete design specifications, architectural whitepapers, mechanical formulas, and development roadmaps that steer all Tellurian custom mods and pack configurations.

---

## 📑 Core Documentation

1. [**Master Design Specification (MDS.md)**](MDS.md)
   - **Section 1: World Topology & Rendering Engine** (Finite toroidal wrapping, horizon curvature shader, pre-generated global LOD).
   - **Section 2: Vertical Cosmology** (The Underworld mantle, Mortal Realm, Shattered Heavens orbital asteroid belt & Void Wyrm boss).
   - **Section 3: Custom Worldgen, Geology & Climate** (Latitudinal Z-axis climate, Banded Iron Formations, finite ore depletion, closed-planet economy safety nets).
   - **Section 4: Autonomous Settlements & Civil Engineering** (Historical worldgen simulation, 2D plot grid zoning, wide surveyed road corridors, modular wall fortresses, vehicle manufacturing).
   - **Section 5: Workforce & Society** (14 lean non-overlapping professions, dedicated housing & sleep schedule, closed-loop wage/food economy).
   - **Section 6: Logistics & Manufacturing** (Universal Material Zone green box, encumbered chest carrying, steampunk vehicle tiers, just-in-time custom artisan crafting, parcel freight routing).
   - **Section 7: Commerce & Communication** (Pure emerald standard, geographic comparative advantage arbitrage, Town Hall mailboxes, maritime shipping lanes).
   - **Section 8: Governance & Real Estate** (Corporate Tycoon vs. Civic Mayor, Town Planning Board UI, buying cities outright, ruin clean takeovers).
   - **Section 9: Multiplayer, Law & Threat Scaling** (Witness-based crime stat, Lawful Citizen vs. Pillager Outlaw faction flip, scaled Pillager threats & Kingpin arc, offline grief protection).

2. [**Technical Milestones & Roadmap**](ROADMAP.md)
   - Technical phases, simulation engine milestones, and pack release roadmap.
